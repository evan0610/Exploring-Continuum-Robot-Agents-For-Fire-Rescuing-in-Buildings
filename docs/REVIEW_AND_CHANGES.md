# Research and design review

## Issues and implemented corrections

1. **Factory viewpoint identity and stage pairing (critical).** Sequential media IDs had been treated as pairs, causing wrong camera labels, stages, and posters. Inspected original slide 13 shape positions / picture associations, native metadata, and decoded video frames. Correct pairs: exterior robot 10 + external 7; interior robot 8 + external 9. Corrected homepage, README links, and source map.
2. **Architecture versus implementation.** The visual feedback loop is a proposed architecture; task footage alone does not establish the complete autonomous stack. Made this distinction explicit in both language versions.
3. **Physical task versus rescue efficacy.** Fire-related miniature targets and mask transport do not establish live-fire suppression or clinical oxygen delivery. Reworded current scope without inventing trial counts or outcomes.
4. **Independent video views.** Paired clips have different durations; preserve independent playback and state the limitation. No synchronized-playback feature is implied.
5. **Language and navigation.** Added English / Chinese controls, a complete Traditional Chinese page, translated dynamic captions and diagrams, and retention of the current demonstration selection when switching language from the demo section.
6. **Typography and information hierarchy.** Improved Chinese body readability and navigation targets. Removed duplicate Chinese summary and duplicate Chinese award label from the Chinese page. Added subtle visual separation for demonstrated behavior versus remaining evaluation.

## Evidence boundaries

Task sequences derive from the original 14-slide presentation. Video identity derives from the original embedded media, not from assumed file ordering. The third-prize award remains linked to CUHK's official sources. The site presents qualitative prototype tasks and proposed system design, not controlled benchmark findings.
